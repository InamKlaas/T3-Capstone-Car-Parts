Problem Statement

Buying and selling car parts is often a frustrating and unreliable experience. Existing channels are fragmented across classifieds sites, social media groups and word-of-mouth referrals, offering little verification of sellers, no guarantee of part authenticity or compatibility, and no structured way to track orders or transactions. Buyers frequently struggle to find the right part for their vehicle from a trustworthy source, while sellers lack a dedicated platform to reach interested buyers efficiently. This results in wasted time, an increased risk of fraud or receiving incorrect parts, and a generally inaccessible market for a genuinely common need.

Proposed Solution

CarPart Marketplace proposes a centralized, role-based platform purpose-built for buying and selling car parts and related vehicle services. Buyers, sellers and admins each get dedicated accounts and workflows, so that listings, orders, transactions and deliveries are all tracked consistently within a single trusted system rather than scattered across informal channels. By tying every part listing to structured vehicle and category data, and every transaction to a clear order and delivery process, the platform makes it straightforward for buyers to find the exact part they need and for sellers to manage their listings and sales in one place.

Business Justification

CarPart Marketplace gives buyers a reliable, searchable source for car parts instead of relying on unverified informal listings, reducing the risk of buying the wrong part or being scammed. It gives sellers — from individual part-sellers to small vendors — a dedicated audience of buyers actively looking for automotive parts, rather than competing for attention on general marketplaces. Structuring transactions, orders and deliveries around defined entities also creates a foundation for building trust and accountability into the platform as it grows.

Expected Benefits

By bringing buyers and sellers together in one dedicated space, the project is expected to make sourcing car parts faster and more reliable. Structured order and transaction tracking gives both sides clearer visibility into the status of a purchase, from order placement through to delivery. Sellers gain a focused channel to list and manage parts and vehicles, while buyers benefit from an organized, role-based system that's easier to navigate than informal alternatives.

Tech Stack

React + Vite (Tailwind CSS, react-router-dom) on the frontend, Java with Spring Boot on the backend, with a MySQL database.
